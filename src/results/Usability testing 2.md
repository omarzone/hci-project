# Usability Testing Report

## Summary
The purpose of this usability test is to evaluate the RF005 and RNF005. This test focuses on understanding how well the bot satisfies these requirements, specifically its usability, performance, and user satisfaction.
Requirement RF005: The bot must be able to change the selected voice to enhance the quality of communication.
Requirement RNF005: Ensure a consistent user experience across different devices and platforms, with uniform behavior, interface design, and response quality.

## Goals
1. Determine the bot's effectiveness in enhancing communication quality through voice-changing capabilities during real-time conversations.
2. Assess the bot’s consistency in user experience across smartphone and laptop platforms.
3. Evaluate user satisfaction with the bot’s performance for both requirements.

## Test Participants
- Number of Participants: 10 students  
- Profile: Students from Universidad Autónoma de Yucatán, native Spanish speakers, intermediate to advanced English proficiency.  
- Devices Used: Smartphone and laptop.  

## Tasks
Task 1: Analyze Voice-Changing Effectiveness (RF005)  
Participants engage in a conversational task where they introduce themselves or explain a concept. The bot demonstrates different voice tones and styles, providing feedback on how these adjustments enhance communication quality.

Scenario: Students pretend to deliver a short speech in English while the bot dynamically changes its voice and evaluates the impact on clarity and emotional tone.

Task 2: Evaluate User Experience Consistency (RNF005)  
Participants perform the same task using the bot on a smartphone and laptop to identify discrepancies in interface design, behavior, and response quality.

Scenario: Students use both devices to complete a set task (e.g., accessing the bot’s voice-changing features and engaging in a conversation module).

## Test Methodology
Procedure  
Introduction:  
Participants are briefed on the purpose of the usability test and the tasks they will complete.

Testing Voice-Changing Effectiveness (Task 1):  
Participants engage in a conversation with the bot, focusing on enhancing communication quality through voice modulation.
The bot provides dynamic adjustments to its voice and feedback on the impact of these changes.

Testing Consistency Across Platforms (Task 2):  
Participants perform the same task on both a smartphone and laptop to evaluate user experience consistency.

Post-Test Questionnaire:  
Participants complete a survey to provide feedback on their experience with the bot.

## Test Metrics
Task 1: Voice-Changing Effectiveness (RF005)  
Feedback Accuracy Rate: Percentage of voice adjustments rated as effective by participants.  
Task Completion Rate: Percentage of participants successfully integrating voice adjustments into their conversations.  
Response Time: Time taken by the bot to implement voice changes after input.  
User Improvement: Changes observed in participants’ communication quality after using the bot.

Task 2: User Experience Consistency (RNF005)  
Consistency Rating: Participant ratings on the similarity of experience across platforms.  
Task Completion Rate: Percentage of participants completing tasks without device-specific difficulties.  
Response Time Difference: Variation in bot response times across devices.  
Error Rate: Number of usability issues encountered on each platform.

## Results
Task 1: Voice-Changing Effectiveness  
Feedback Accuracy Rate: 88% of voice adjustments were rated as effective or very effective.  
Task Completion Rate: 83% of participants successfully integrated voice adjustments into their conversations.  
Response Time: Average response time for voice changes was 4.5 seconds.  
User Improvement: 72% of participants reported noticeable enhancements in their communication quality.

Task 2: User Experience Consistency  
Consistency Rating: 90% of participants rated the experience as consistent or very consistent.  
Task Completion Rate: 94% of participants completed the task on both devices without issues.  
Response Time Difference: Average response time difference was 7%.  
Error Rate: Only 4% of participants reported minor usability issues.

## Recommendations
1. Improve Voice Adjustment Personalization:  
While most participants found voice changes effective, adjustments can be further tailored to suit different conversational contexts.

2. Enhance Mobile Interface:  
A few participants found navigating voice-change features on the smartphone more challenging.

3. Reduce Response Times:  
Optimize response algorithms to ensure voice changes are implemented in under 4 seconds.
