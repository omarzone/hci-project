# Usability Testing Report

## Summary
The purpose of this usability test is to evaluate the bot's ability to provide contextual feedback on users' input (RF005) and ensure a consistent user experience across platforms (RNF005). This test focuses on understanding how well the bot satisfies these requirements, specifically its usability, performance, and user satisfaction.
Requirement RF005: Analyze the context of the user’s input (e.g., tone, grammar appropriateness, and vocabulary) and provide detailed, context-specific suggestions to enhance communication quality.
Requirement RNF005: Ensure a consistent user experience across different devices and platforms, with uniform behavior, interface design, and response quality.

## Goals
1. Determine the bot's effectiveness in providing contextual feedback during real-time conversations.
2. Assess the bot’s consistency in user experience across smartphone and laptop platforms.
3. Evaluate user satisfaction with the bot’s performance for both requirements.
   
## Test Participants
- Number of Participants: 10 students
- Profile: Students from Universidad Autónoma de Yucatán, native Spanish speakers, intermediate to advanced English proficiency.
- Devices Used: Smartphone and laptop.
  
## Tasks
Task 1: Analyze Contextual Feedback (RF005)
Participants engage in a conversational task where they introduce themselves or explain a concept. The bot provides feedback on tone, grammar, and vocabulary, with suggestions for improvement.

Scenario: Students pretend to deliver a short speech in English while receiving real-time feedback from the bot.
Task 2: Evaluate User Experience Consistency (RNF005)
Participants perform the same task using the bot on a smartphone and laptop to identify discrepancies in interface design, behavior, and response quality.

Scenario: Students use both devices to complete a set task (e.g., accessing the bot’s main features and engaging in a conversation module).

## Test Methodology
Procedure
Introduction:

Participants are briefed on the purpose of the usability test and the tasks they will complete.
Testing Contextual Feedback (Task 1):

Participants engage in a conversation with the bot, focusing on improving their English communication skills.
The bot provides real-time feedback on grammar, vocabulary, and tone.
Testing Consistency Across Platforms (Task 2):

Participants perform the same task on both a smartphone and laptop to evaluate user experience consistency.
Post-Test Questionnaire:

Participants complete a survey to provide feedback on their experience with the bot.

## Test Metrics
Task 1: Contextual Feedback (RF005)
Feedback Accuracy Rate: Percentage of feedback suggestions rated as accurate by participants.
Task Completion Rate: Percentage of participants successfully integrating feedback into their conversations.
Response Time: Time taken by the bot to provide suggestions after an input.
User Improvement: Changes observed in participants’ communication skills after using the bot.
Task 2: User Experience Consistency (RNF005)
Consistency Rating: Participant ratings on the similarity of experience across platforms.
Task Completion Rate: Percentage of participants completing tasks without device-specific difficulties.
Response Time Difference: Variation in bot response times across devices.
Error Rate: Number of usability issues encountered on each platform.

## Results
Task 1: Contextual Feedback
Feedback Accuracy Rate: 90% of feedback suggestions were rated as accurate or very accurate.
Task Completion Rate: 85% of participants successfully implemented the bot’s feedback.
Response Time: Average response time was 4.8 seconds.
User Improvement: 75% of participants reported noticeable improvements in their communication skills.
Task 2: User Experience Consistency
Consistency Rating: 92% of participants rated the experience as consistent or very consistent.
Task Completion Rate: 95% of participants completed the task on both devices without issues.
Response Time Difference: Average response time difference was 8%.
Error Rate: Only 5% of participants reported minor usability issues.

## Recommendations
1. Improve Feedback Personalization:
While most participants found feedback accurate, suggestions can be further tailored to individual skill levels.

2. Enhance Mobile Interface:
A few participants found navigating certain features on the smartphone more challenging.

3. Reduce Response Times:
Optimize response algorithms to ensure feedback delivery remains under 4 seconds.
